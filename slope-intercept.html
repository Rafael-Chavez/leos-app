📈 Slope-Intercept Form

📚 Learning: Slope-Intercept Form

y = mx + b

What does each part mean?

  • y = the y-coordinate (up and down)
  • m = the slope (how steep the line is)
  • x = the x-coordinate (left and right)
  • b = the y-intercept (where the line crosses the y-axis)

Understanding Slope (m)

The slope tells you how steep the line is and which direction it goes:

  • Positive slope (like m = 2): The line goes UP from left to right ↗️
  • Negative slope (like m = -3): The line goes DOWN from left to right ↘️
  • Zero slope (m = 0): The line is horizontal (flat) →
  • Undefined slope: The line is vertical ↑

The slope is the "rise over run" = change in y / change in x

Understanding Y-Intercept (b)

The y-intercept is where the line crosses the y-axis (the vertical line).

  • This happens when x = 0
  • If b = 3, the line crosses the y-axis at point (0, 3)
  • If b = -2, the line crosses the y-axis at point (0, -2)

Example 1: Writing an Equation from Slope and Y-Intercept

Problem: Write the equation of a line with slope = 2 and y-intercept = -3

Solution:

  • Start with y = mx + b
  • m = 2 (the slope)
  • b = -3 (the y-intercept)
  • Substitute: y = 2x + (-3)
  • Answer: y = 2x - 3

Example 2: Finding Slope and Y-Intercept from an Equation

Problem: Find the slope and y-intercept of y = -4x + 7

Solution:

  • Compare to y = mx + b
  • The number with x is the slope: m = -4
  • The number by itself is the y-intercept: b = 7

Example 3: Rewriting to Slope-Intercept Form

Problem: Rewrite 2x + y = 6 in slope-intercept form

Solution:

  • Goal: Get y by itself on one side
  • 2x + y = 6
  • Subtract 2x from both sides: y = -2x + 6
  • Answer: y = -2x + 6 (slope = -2, y-intercept = 6)

Example 4: Finding Equation from a Graph

Steps to find the equation from a graph:

  1. Find the y-intercept (b): Look where the line crosses the y-axis
  2. Find the slope (m): Pick two points on the line and count:
    • Rise = how many units up or down
    • Run = how many units left or right
    • Slope = rise / run
  3. Write the equation: y = mx + b

Example: If a line crosses the y-axis at 4, and from there goes up 3 and right 2 to the next point:

  • b = 4 (y-intercept)
  • m = 3/2 (rise/run = 3/2)
  • Answer: y = (3/2)x + 4 or y = 1.5x + 4

💡 Quick Tips:

  • Always get y by itself on the left side
  • The number with x is ALWAYS the slope
  • The number without x is ALWAYS the y-intercept
  • Use fractions or decimals for slope
  • When counting slope on a graph, always go from left to right
